The arbitrage estimate assumes the fund qualifies as an equity-oriented fund under the Income Tax rules and that required conditions, including securities transaction tax where applicable, are met. For transfers on or after 23 July 2024 it applies: short-term gains at 20%, long-term gains at 12.5% on the amount above the ₹1.25 lakh annual threshold, which is shared across all of your eligible long-term gains in the financial year, plus 4% health and education cess.
